Built for US investors

Move from scattered tabs to one clean US market command center.

Designed for US investors who want a faster way to monitor NYSE and Nasdaq holdings — S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow always in view — without interrupting the work already on their screen.

↗

Live ticker tape

Keep prices, daily moves, and indices in view on any page. Pause, adjust speed from 0.5x to 3.0x, or glide through the tape with smooth controls.

The tape reads your saved watchlist from local storage, so indices and your own stocks scroll together. Click any ticker for a detail popup with its chart.

◌

Markets tab · US-first + 9 more

Start with USA — S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, top stocks and macro data — then switch to 9 more economies for indices, top stocks and macro data.

Switch economies without losing your watchlist — your portfolios stay intact in the side panel while you browse global macro data.

▦

Heatmap, gainers & losers

Index heatmap plus strictly separated top gainers and losers per market. Click anything for full analysis.

Gainers and losers are strictly separated lists — never a mixed table — and clicking any row opens its full analysis.

◈

Economy indicators

Live macro stats — inflation, unemployment, GDP growth and GDP per capita — with editable tiles and World Bank detail pages.

Tiles are editable per economy and every tile links to its World Bank source page, so you can verify each number at the source.

⌁

Interactive charts

1M to MAX charts with hover price-and-date crosshair and period returns that recalculate as you switch ranges.

Ranges run from 1M to MAX with a hover crosshair showing the exact price and date, plus period returns that recalculate every time you switch ranges.

✦

Signal-rich updates

Live green and red movement highlights, prices, market caps, P/E, AI verdicts and price alerts without leaving your tab.

Alerts run on background alarms with desktop notifications, and green/red movement highlights update live as prices stream in.

True US prices

US prices stay in dollars. Global markets stay native.

US stocks, ETFs and indices — S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow — display in $. When you check other economies, each market keeps its native currency, including ₹, ¥, £, €, S$, HK$, A$ and C$. No silent conversion, ever.
