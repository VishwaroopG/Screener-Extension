01 Watchlist

Add to Watchlist

Right-click any stock name or ticker symbol on any webpage to instantly add it to your Screener watchlist. No copy-pasting required.

Tap the P/E header to switch the same column to Div Yld, ROCE, ROE, or P/B - choice persists, P/B auto-derived so Indian stocks never show blank, ratios render Screener-style (51.5 not 51.50). Dot-tag holdings with color tags, filter by tag, and let Smart Focus blue-dot big moves (>5%), unusual swings, or near 52-week highs/lows - hit Focus for firing stocks only, mute per stock. Bulk Select mode adds header Select-All with tag / move / delete across portfolios without losing scroll.

02 Markets

USA + 9 more

Opens on the USA. S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow. Switch economies without losing your watchlist. Track the FTSE 100 in the UK, Nifty 50 in India, Nikkei 225 in Japan, Hang Seng in Hong Kong, DAX in Germany, CAC 40 in France, ASX 200 in Australia, TSX in Canada, and the Straits Times Index in Singapore. Each economy displays its native currency with live macro indicators tied to World Bank data.

Whole-market advance / decline breadth sits above gainers and losers: Nifty 500 totals for India and combined NYSE + Nasdaq totals for the USA, so you see true market participation instead of a partial count. A Market Mood gauge tops the tab: the live Tickertape Market Mood Index for India and the CNN Fear & Greed index for the USA, with a data-driven proxy for other economies - hover any arc for its zone, tap the ⓘ for the factors behind the number. Portfolios stay intact in the side panel while you browse global macro data. Switch between any two economies instantly your watchlist, alerts, and chart preferences carry over without reset. Macro tiles like inflation, GDP, and unemployment update per economy so you always see the context behind the numbers.

03 Heatmap

Gainers & losers, separated

Whole-market breadth bar plus index heatmap plus strictly separated gainers and losers. Click anything for full analysis. The heatmap renders every stock in the index as a colour-coded tile sized by market cap. Red shades mark gains, green shades mark losses, and neutral grey sits in between. A single click opens the stock detail popup with sparkline chart, period returns, P/E ratio, market cap, and AI verdict all without leaving the page.

Heatmap tiles update in real time as prices stream in. Hover any tile to see the exact percentage move and current price. The layout reflows automatically when the index composition changes, keeping the view tight and readable even on smaller screens.

04 Macro

Economy indicators with sources

Inflation, unemployment, GDP growth and GDP per capita. Editable tiles, every tile linked to its World Bank page.

05 Charts

1M to MAX, hover crosshair

Exact price-and-date on hover, period returns recalculated per range.

Tape popups now harden 1M/6M/1Y/3Y/5Y/10Y/MAX buttons against any host-site CSS so they never stack vertically, and long company descriptions get a Read more / Read less toggle only when actually clamped.

06 Signals

Alerts without leaving your tab

Green / red movement highlights, market cap, P/E, AI verdicts and background price alerts with desktop notifications. Every price tick is evaluated against your alert thresholds in the background. When a condition is met whether a percentage swing, a market-cap milestone, or a P/E level a desktop notification fires instantly, even when the side panel is closed.

Alerts run on background alarms with desktop notifications as prices stream in. Movement highlights paint green or red on the ticker row the moment a threshold is crossed. AI verdicts synthesize price action, volume and fundamentals into a concise buy / hold / watch signal. You stay informed without refreshing, switching tabs, or losing focus on what you are reading.

07 Import

One-click Kite / Screener / TradingView import

Bring holdings over from Zerodha Kite, Screener.in, or TradingView - auto-detect tickers from the open tab (even login-walled pages), paste a list, or upload a holdings CSV or TXT file. Every ticker resolves automatically and merges into your active portfolio, skipping duplicates, with a checkbox preview and live selection counts.

Nothing is read in the background: tickers are read only at the moment you click Detect, shown for confirmation, and added only after you approve. Portfolio dropdown sits beside Your Watchlist for faster switching after import.

08 Tape

Silk tape, same speed everywhere

Time-based scroll engine moves at identical px/sec on 60Hz, 120Hz, and throttled tabs, with a seamless loop in both left and right directions. Flash highlights are background-only so live ticks never jitter or shift marquee width mid-scroll - DOM updates only when values actually change.

Speed popover is fixed-position so header overflow never clips 2.0x+ presets, closes with ESC, and all native dropdowns (trend, ratio, portfolio, markets) render readable in dark mode.

09 Fundamentals Depth - new in v2.6.10

SWOT, checklist, red-flags on every search

Search any stock - RELIANCE, HDFCBANK, AAPL - and get Tickertape-parity depth free: a Checklist score out of 10, SWOT built from Screener pros/cons, forensic red-flags (low ROE, high P/E with low ROCE, weak payout, pledge/contingent/debtor keywords), promoter / FII / DII / public shareholding with quarterly trend, concall transcripts with PPT and AI-summary links, plus one-click shortcuts to Screener report, annual reports, Tickertape and Trendlyne Superstars. Hit PDF to save the card. Indian equities parse live from Screener.in; global stocks get Yahoo fundamentals with the same layout.

Why free? Tickertape locks score, rank and forecasts behind Pro. Here the checklist, SWOT and flags stay in the side panel - no paywall, no new tab.
