Ticker Screener turns every browser tab into a focused market workspace — a live watchlist, a 10-economy Markets tab, interactive price charts, and a ticker tape that moves with you.

LIVE WATCHLIST & PORTFOLIOS
- Track Indian (NSE/BSE) and global stocks with live prices, day change, sparkline trend, P/E, market cap, dividend yield and ROCE.
- Multiple portfolios with drag-to-reorder tickers, CSV export, and rename support.
- AI verdict on every stock (Undervalued / Solid / Expensive / Risk) from P/E and capital efficiency.
- Price alerts with desktop notifications when a target is crossed.

10-ECONOMY MARKETS TAB
- USA, India, UK, Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Germany, France, Australia, Canada.
- Index heatmap plus strictly separated top gainers and losers per market.
- Live macro indicators (inflation, unemployment, GDP growth, GDP per capita and more) with World Bank sources.

TICKER TAPE THAT MOVES WITH YOU
- Live prices on any page. Pause, 0.5x–3.0x speed control, top/bottom position, 3 themes, 3 sizes, scroll direction, pause-on-hover.
- Show/hide indices, % change or amount change. Disable per-site.

INTERACTIVE CHARTS
- 1M to MAX ranges with hover price-and-date crosshair. The line turns green when the period return is positive and red when negative, with period returns recalculated per range.

WORLD MARKET CLOCKS
- Draggable market clock tiles (New York, London, Mumbai, Hong Kong, Singapore and 10 more). Tiles sort by local time by default; click any tile to swap its market.

ALSO INCLUDED
- Right-click any company name on the web to add it to your watchlist.
- Dark/light mode, 55 interface languages, cross-device sync via your browser account.
- No account needed. Your data stays in your browser (local storage only).

DATA SOURCES
Indian ratios: Screener.in. Prices/indices/charts: Yahoo Finance. Fundamentals (US): Finviz. Macro: World Bank. News: Google News RSS.
