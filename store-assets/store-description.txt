Ticker Screener: Live Watchlist, Global Markets & Ticker Tape
Your market workspace, always in sight. Ticker Screener turns your browser into a powerful, real-time financial dashboard. It features a live watchlist for US and Indian stocks, crypto, forex pairs, and commodities, a persistent scrolling ticker tape across web pages, interactive charts, and a 10-economy Global Markets hub.

Stocks, ETFs, indices, crypto, and forex display in their native trading currencies ($ for US markets and crypto, ₹ for Indian equities, native currencies globally). No silent or confusing currency conversions.

Available in 10 languages: English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Arabic, and Russian.

🔥 KEY FEATURES

📈 1. Live Watchlists & Multi-Portfolio Management
Real-Time Price Action: Live price ticks, 1-day change, 1-year sparklines, P/E ratios, market capitalization (now with currency symbols), dividend yield, and ROCE flashing green/red with market momentum.
Switchable Ratio Column: Tap the P/E header to instantly switch the same column to Dividend Yield, ROCE, ROE, or P/B. Your choice persists. P/B is auto-derived for Indian stocks (Price / Book Value) so it never shows blank.
One-Tap Add & Remove: Add a stock and remove it again directly in the search results, with no need to scroll down to your watchlist.
Multiple Portfolios: Organize your holdings into custom portfolios with drag-and-drop reordering, column sorting, and instant 1-click CSV export to Excel.
Color Tags + Tag Filter: Dot-tag any holding, filter the watchlist to one tag, bulk-tag many at once. Explicit tags always win; clearing returns to neutral grey.
Smart Focus Highlights: Blue dot auto-marks big moves (>5%), unusual intraday swings, or stocks near 52-week highs/lows. Hit Focus to see only firing stocks, mute per-stock, restore anytime.
Bulk Edit Mode: Select checkbox mode with header Select-All, bulk tag / move / copy / delete across portfolios. Horizontal scroll position is preserved on live re-renders.
Screener-Accurate Ratios: 51.5 not 51.50, with bank/insurer and standalone-only fallbacks, so Indian ratios match Screener.in exactly.
Price Alerts: Set custom target alerts in each asset's native currency with real-time desktop push notifications so you never miss an entry or exit.
AI Valuation Verdict: Automated valuation indicators (Undervalued / Solid / Expensive / High Risk) computed from P/E and return ratios, complete with peer comparisons and company updates.

📥 2. One-Click Watchlist Import
Bring Your Existing Holdings: Import watchlists from Zerodha Kite, Screener.in, TradingView, or any broker — no rebuilding from scratch.
Detect From Open Tab: Open your Kite watchlist, Screener.in watchlist or screen, or TradingView watchlist and import its tickers in one click. Tickers are read from the tab only at the moment you click Detect, shown in a checkbox preview, and nothing is added without your confirmation.
Paste or Upload: Paste tickers separated by commas, spaces, or newlines, or upload a holdings CSV or TXT export.
Smart Resolve & Merge: Every ticker is automatically resolved to its canonical symbol and merged into your active portfolio, skipping duplicates. Preview everything with checkboxes and a live selected-count before adding.

🪙 3. Crypto, Forex & Commodities
Beyond Stocks: Track major coins, currency pairs, and commodities like Gold, Silver, and Crude Oil, all powered by live quotes.
Full Citizens: Every asset gets live prices, day ranges, 1-year sparklines, price alerts, and ticker-tape support, just like stocks.
Smart Precision: Sub-dollar assets display up to 6 decimals, and suggestions are badged by asset type so you always know what you're adding.

🎞️ 4. Floating Ticker Tape (Follows You Everywhere)
Always Visible: Keep an eye on your custom watchlist alongside major benchmarks, scrolling unobtrusively on any web page you visit.
Silk Scroll Engine: Time-based animation runs at identical speed on 60Hz, 120Hz, and throttled tabs, with a seamless loop in both left and right directions. Flash highlights are background-only so prices never jitter or shift layout mid-scroll.
Fully Customizable: Pin to top or bottom; choose Compact, Comfortable, or Large sizing; switch between Dark, Light, or Blue themes.
Complete Control: Adjustable scroll speed (0.5x to 3.0x) with fixed popover + ESC to close, pause on mouse hover, one-click pause/play toggles, and per-site disable controls.
Flexible Readouts: Display percentage change, absolute amount change, or both combined.
Unbreakable Stock Popups: Chart 1M/6M/1Y/3Y/5Y/10Y/MAX buttons stay in a row on any site, company description gets Read more / Read less only when actually clamped.

🌐 5. 10-Economy Global Markets Hub
Worldwide Coverage: Seamlessly switch between the USA, India, United Kingdom, Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Germany, France, Australia, and Canada.
Market Mood Gauge: A Fear/Greed dial tops the Markets tab — the live Tickertape Market Mood Index (MMI) for India and the CNN Fear & Greed index for the USA, with a market-data proxy for other economies. Hover any arc for its zone (Extreme Fear to Extreme Greed); the ⓘ icon lists the exact factors behind the number.
Whole-Market Advance / Decline Breadth: A green/red participation bar above gainers and losers shows true market totals — Nifty 500 advances vs declines for India, combined NYSE + Nasdaq for the USA. No partial watchlist counts; economies without a verified whole-market feed show no bar rather than a misleading number.
Market Heatmaps: Instantly spot momentum with clearly separated Top Gainers and Top Losers.
Customizable Benchmark Cards: Track 35+ preset indices, major commodities (Gold, Crude Oil, Silver), and currency pairs. Tap any card to add it to your watchlist.

📊 6. Verifiable Macroeconomic Indicators
Real Economic Data: Track year-over-year CPI Inflation, Unemployment, GDP Growth, and GDP per Capita.
World Bank Integration: Swap indicators to inspect National Debt, Trade Balance, Forex Reserves, or Population metrics. Every single tile links directly to its verified World Bank source data.

📉 7. Interactive Charts
Responsive Timeframes: 1M, 6M, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, 10Y, and MAX historical trend charts.
Hover Crosshair: Pinpoint precise historical closing prices and dates on hover.
Adaptive Trend Color: Dynamically shifts between green (net positive return) and red (net negative return) based on the selected period.

🕒 8. Market Clocks & Curated Financial News
World Market Clocks: Choose from 15 international markets with live Open / Closed indicators and automated holiday-calendar detection. Drag to reorder.
Targeted Portfolio News: Live news feeds tailored strictly to your saved portfolio holdings, paired with macro economic headlines and publisher thumbnails.

💬 9. Feedback That Reaches Us
In-Panel Feedback: Send bug reports and feature ideas without leaving the extension. Messages land straight in our inbox, with an optional email if you'd like a reply. Only what you type and hit Send on ever leaves the extension.
Rate Us: Enjoying Ticker Screener? A store rating from the panel footer takes ten seconds and keeps development going.

🔒 PRIVACY — YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account, no ads, no tracking: No sign-ups, no advertising SDKs, no analytics. We never sell your data or build advertising profiles.
Stored locally: Your watchlists, portfolios, alerts, and preferences stay in your browser's local storage, with optional sync across your own signed-in devices through your browser profile. There is no developer server holding your data.
What leaves your browser, and why: (1) Ticker symbols you look up are sent to market-data providers (Yahoo Finance, Screener.in, Finviz, Moneycontrol, WSJ, Tickertape, CNN, World Bank, Google News) so they can return your quotes — that is how any market app fetches prices. (2) Feedback you write is emailed to the developer only when you hit Send. (3) Your signed-in profile email is shown inside Settings so you can verify which browser profile your preferences sync to — it is never transmitted to our servers. (4) The per-site ticker switch reads only the website's domain name to respect your on/off choice — never page content or browsing history.
Full transparency: the Privacy Policy linked on this listing discloses all user-data collection, storage, and sharing, and every party data is shared with.
Zero remote code: 100% packaged locally and strictly adheres to Google Chrome Manifest V3 security standards.

🚀 HOW TO USE
Press Ctrl + Shift + S (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + S (Mac) to open the side panel instantly. Search any stock, crypto, forex pair, or commodity to build your watchlist — or click Import to bring holdings over from Zerodha Kite, Screener.in, or TradingView. Open the Markets tab to check the Market Mood gauge, breadth, global heatmaps, macroeconomic data, and world market clocks. Click the Gear (⚙) icon to customize your scrolling ticker tape (position, size, theme, speed). Click the ⛶ (Full-Screen) icon anytime to expand the side panel into a full-tab workspace.

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Is the price data live? Prices stream with live background polling and instant in-tab updates. As with any market data feed, some exchanges may be slightly delayed.
Does it work on all websites? Yes. The ticker tape overlays any standard webpage, and the watchlist lives in the browser side panel (or a full tab), so there is no tab-switching to finance sites.
Can I import my existing watchlist from Zerodha or Screener.in? Yes. Click Import in the watchlist header, then detect tickers from your open Kite, Screener.in, or TradingView tab, paste a list, or upload a holdings CSV. Tickers resolve automatically and merge into your active portfolio without duplicates.
Can I track crypto, forex, and commodities? Yes. Major coins, currency pairs, gold, oil, and more get live prices, charts, alerts, and tape support like any stock.
Is it really free? Completely free, no account, no premium tier, no time limits.
What data do you collect? Your watchlists and settings stay in your browser (optionally synced across your own signed-in devices). Ticker symbols you search are sent to the data providers below so they can return your quotes. Your profile email is displayed locally in Settings only, and feedback is sent only when you hit Send. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for ads. Full details are in the Privacy Policy linked on this listing.
Why do prices show $, ₹, and other symbols? Every asset displays in its native trading currency so you always know what you're looking at, never a silent conversion.
How is this different from price-only ticker extensions? Ticker Screener pairs live prices with fundamentals (P/E, ROCE, market cap, dividends), AI valuation verdicts, price alerts, macro data, and news. It is a workspace, not just a number strip.
