Ticker Screener: Live Watchlist, Markets & Ticker Tape
Your market, always in sight. Ticker Screener turns every browser tab into a market workspace — a live NYSE / Nasdaq watchlist, an S&P 500 / Nasdaq / Dow ticker tape, interactive charts, and a 10-economy Markets tab for global context.

Stocks, ETFs and indices display in the currency they trade in — $ for Wall Street, and native currencies everywhere else. No silent conversion, ever.

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.5.1
- Full-screen view: hit the fullscreen button in the panel header to open the whole workspace in a dedicated browser tab.
- Combined tape readout: show % change and amount change together, like +$5.20 (+0.37%).
- Dashboard refresh button, publisher thumbnails in News, and a right-click menu that shows just the stock name.
- The Markets tab opens on the USA — S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, top stocks and macro data.

LIVE WATCHLIST & PORTFOLIOS
- Live price, day change, 1Y sparkline, P/E, market cap, dividend yield and ROCE, flashing green/red on every tick.
- Multiple portfolios with drag-to-reorder, sort, rename and one-click CSV export to Excel.
- Price alerts with desktop notifications when your target is hit.
- AI Verdict on every stock (Undervalued / Solid / Expensive / High Risk) from P/E + ROCE, plus peer comparison and company announcements.

TICKER TAPE THAT MOVES WITH YOU
- Your watchlist plus S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow, scrolling on every website you visit.
- Pin to top or bottom; Compact, Comfortable or Large; Dark, Light or Blue theme.
- 0.5x–3.0x speed, scroll direction, pause on hover, pause/resume controls.
- Show % change, amount change, or both combined. Hide the tape or disable it per site.

10-ECONOMY MARKETS TAB
- Opens on the USA, plus India, UK, Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Germany, France, Australia and Canada.
- Index heatmap with strictly separated Top Gainers and Top Losers — click anything for full analysis.
- 4 customizable index cards with 35+ presets: indices, commodities and currencies.

ECONOMY INDICATORS YOU CAN VERIFY
- Inflation (CPI), Unemployment, GDP Growth and GDP per Capita, each year-labeled.
- Click any tile to swap in GDP Total, Trade, Forex Reserves or Population — every tile links to its World Bank source.

INTERACTIVE CHARTS
- 1M to MAX ranges with a hover crosshair for exact price and date.
- The line turns green when the period return is positive, red when negative.

NEWS & WORLD MARKET CLOCKS
- Portfolio news grouped by your holdings, plus US macro news, with publisher thumbnails.
- Draggable clock tiles for 15 markets with holiday-aware Open/Closed status.

ALSO INCLUDED
- Search by company name or ticker; right-click any company name on the web to add it instantly.
- Cross-device sync through your browser account — no separate account needed.
- Dark / light mode, 55 languages, side-panel shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+S / Cmd+Shift+S).
- No account, no backend: your data stays in your browser.

HOW TO USE
1. Press Ctrl+Shift+S (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+S (Mac) to open the sidebar.
2. Search a ticker (e.g. AAPL, NVDA, RELIANCE.NS) and add it to your watchlist.
3. Open the Markets tab and explore heatmap, gainers/losers and macro data.
4. Click the gear icon for Tape Settings: position, size, theme, speed and data display.
5. Hit the fullscreen button anytime for the full-screen tab view.

DATA SOURCES
Prices, indices & charts: Yahoo Finance. US fundamentals: Finviz (with Yahoo Finance fallback). Indian ratios: Screener.in. Macro: World Bank. News: Google News RSS.
