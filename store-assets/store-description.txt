Ticker Screener: Live US Watchlist, Markets & Ticker Tape
Your market, always in sight. Turn every browser tab into a focused market workspace — live NYSE / Nasdaq watchlist, S&P 500 / Nasdaq / Dow ticker tape, interactive charts, plus global context when you need it.

US stocks, ETFs and indices display in true $ prices. When you check other economies, each market keeps its native currency — no silent conversion, ever.

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.5.1
- Full-screen view: hit the ⛶ button in the panel header to open the whole workspace in a dedicated browser tab.
- Combined tape readout: show % change and amount change together like +$5.20 (+0.37%).
- Dashboard refresh button, publisher thumbnails in News, and right-click menu now shows just the stock name.
- The Markets tab opens on the USA with S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, top US stocks and US macro.
- Holiday-aware market clocks: Open/Closed status now respects real 2026 exchange holidays for 15 markets, auto-updated with no extension update needed.
- Right-click to add: select any company name on any webpage → Add to Ticker Screener Watchlist.
- 55 interface languages, refined dark mode.

LIVE WATCHLIST & PORTFOLIOS
- Track NYSE, Nasdaq and global stocks with live price, day change, 1Y sparkline, P/E, market cap, dividend yield and ROCE.
- Prices flash green/red on every tick so momentum is visible instantly.
- Multiple portfolios, drag-to-reorder, sort by price or market cap, rename, CSV export to Excel.
- Price alerts with desktop notifications when your target is crossed.
- AI Verdict on every stock (Undervalued / Solid / Expensive / High Risk) from P/E + ROCE, plus peer comparison and company announcements.

TICKER TAPE THAT MOVES WITH YOU
- Live S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow + your own watchlist on every website you browse.
- Pin to Top or Bottom. Compact, Comfortable or Large sizes. Dark, Light and Blue themes.
- 0.5x–3.0x speed control, scroll Left/Right, Pause on Hover, pause/resume controls.
- Show/hide indices; show % change, amount change, or both combined like +5.20 (+0.37%). Hide temporarily or disable completely on specific sites.

10-ECONOMY MARKETS TAB
- Opens on the USA, plus India, UK, Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Germany, France, Australia, Canada.
- Index heatmap + strictly separated Top Gainers and Top Losers — a negative stock never appears under gainers.
- Click any index or stock for full analysis with charts and fundamentals.
- 4 customizable index cards with 35+ presets: indices, commodities and currencies. Just type to find.

ECONOMY INDICATORS WITH REAL SOURCES
- Live macro per market: Inflation (CPI), Unemployment, GDP Growth, GDP per Capita — each year-labeled.
- Click any tile to swap in GDP Total, Trade, Forex Reserves or Population.
- Every tile links directly to its World Bank source.

INTERACTIVE CHARTS
- 1M, 6M, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, 10Y, MAX with hover crosshair showing exact price and date.
- Line turns green when period return is positive, red when negative. Returns recalculate as you switch ranges.

NEWS & WORLD MARKET CLOCKS
- Portfolio news grouped by your holdings + dedicated US Macro News.
- Draggable clock tiles for 15 markets (New York, London, Mumbai, Hong Kong, Singapore + more), auto-sorted by local time.
- Holiday-aware Open/Closed status. Click any tile to swap its market.

ALSO INCLUDED
- Smart search by company name or ticker for US and international stocks.
- Cross-device Cloud Sync via your browser account (Google / Firefox) — no separate account needed.
- Dark / Light mode, 55 languages, side-panel shortcut.
- No account, no backend. Your data stays in your browser (local storage only).

HOW TO USE
1. Press Ctrl+Shift+S (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+S (Mac) to open the sidebar.
2. Search a ticker (e.g. AAPL, NVDA, RELIANCE.NS) and add it to your watchlist.
3. Open the Markets tab — start with USA, then explore 9 more economies, heatmap, gainers/losers and macro.
4. Click the gear icon for Tape Settings: position, size, theme, speed, and data display.
5. Right-click any company name on the web to add it instantly.
6. Sign into your browser to auto-sync watchlists, alerts and settings across devices.

DATA SOURCES
