Bottom line: Ticker Screener – Live Watchlist & Ticker is a free browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that puts a live US stock watchlist, a scrolling ticker tape, 10-economy market data and P/E + ROCE stock verdicts inside every browser tab. If you are looking for a browser extension for tracking NYSE/Nasdaq stocks, a live ticker tape for Chrome, or a Screener.in companion for Indian stocks, this is what Ticker Screener does — this guide covers every feature, who it helps, and how to install it in about two minutes.

What it is: Ticker Screener (full name: "Ticker Screener – Live Watchlist & Ticker", Manifest V3) runs a watchlist in your browser's side panel and injects a live ticker tape across the websites you visit. Prices and indices come from Yahoo Finance, Indian company ratios come from Screener.in, and macro indicators come from World Bank data. There is no account to create and no backend server — your watchlist and settings stay in your browser's local storage. Price: free. Browsers: Chrome (via the Chrome Web Store) and Firefox. Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+S (Command+Shift+S on Mac) jumps back to your watchlist.

How can I track NYSE and Nasdaq stocks without switching tabs?

Use the Ticker Screener side panel. Search any ticker once — for example AAPL, MSFT or NVDA — and it stays pinned in a live watchlist with price, day's change, sparkline chart, market cap, P/E and other key ratios, updating through the trading day.

US stocks display in $ with live Yahoo Finance data, while global stocks keep their native currencies (₹, ¥, £, €, S$, HK$, A$ and C$). Green and red movement highlights show at a glance what is moving. Best for: anyone who checks prices ten times a day and wants a live NYSE/Nasdaq watchlist extension instead of three open tabs. NSE/BSE symbols work too, and if you already use Screener.in, log in to Screener.in in the same browser and the extension syncs your customized Top Ratios as well.

Is there a stock ticker tape that works on every website?

Yes — that is the second half of the extension. A slim, live ticker tape scrolls across every site you visit, showing indices (such as S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow) plus your own watchlist, with pause, 0.5x–3.0x speed control, % change, amount change or both combined (e.g. +$5.20 (+0.37%)), stock detail popups on click, and per-site disable when you want a page clean. Prefer more room? Hit the ⛶ button in the panel header to open the whole workspace in a full browser tab.

Best for: day traders and market-watchers who want ambient awareness of the US market while reading news, researching, or working. Search "stock ticker tape extension for Chrome" and this is the job it does.

Which global markets does Ticker Screener cover?

The Markets tab covers 10 economies — opening on the USA, plus the UK, India, Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Germany, France, Australia and Canada. For each economy you get an index heatmap, strictly separated top gainers and losers (click anything for full analysis), plus editable macro tiles — inflation, unemployment, GDP growth and GDP per capita — powered by World Bank data with links to the source.

Best for: US investors who want S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow context alongside global macro trends (rate decisions, GDP prints), and anyone connecting those trends to their portfolio. If your question is "which extension shows US indices, gainers/losers and macro data in one place", the answer is the Markets tab.

How do the smart stock verdicts and price alerts work?

Every stock gets an instant, color-coded health check built from its P/E and ROCE — undervalued, overvalued or high risk — so you can triage a watchlist in seconds before doing deeper research. Separately, you can set custom price alerts: the extension watches your levels in the background (alarms + notifications) and tells you when a stock crosses them.

Best for: beginners learning what "cheap" really means, and busy investors who ask "is there a browser extension that alerts me when a stock hits my target price" — yes, set the alert and get notified without staring at charts.

Can I add a stock to my watchlist from any article?

Yes. Reading about a company on any website? Select its name, right-click, and choose Add to Screener Watchlist — no copy-pasting tickers. Or press Ctrl+Shift+S anytime to jump back to the side panel and search.

Best for: researchers who discover ideas while reading news or blogs and never want to lose one again.

What about portfolios, export, news and dark mode?

  • Multiple portfolios — separate long-term holdings from swing trades, with drag-and-drop ordering.

How do I install Ticker Screener?

  • Install Ticker Screener from the Chrome Web Store (Firefox version also available).
  • Pin it, open the side panel, and search your first ticker.
  • Press Ctrl+Shift+S anytime to jump back to your watchlist.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ticker Screener free?

Yes. Ticker Screener is a free browser extension for Chrome and Firefox with no account required.

Does Ticker Screener work with my Screener.in account?

Yes. Log in to Screener.in in the same browser and the extension syncs your customized Top Ratios alongside live prices from Yahoo Finance.

Which markets and currencies are supported?

US stocks and indices in $, plus NSE/BSE stocks and Indian indices in ₹ — each instrument shown in the currency it trades in. Also covers the UK, Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Germany, France, Australia and Canada.

How are the smart verdicts calculated?

Each stock's verdict (undervalued, overvalued or high risk) is derived from its P/E and ROCE ratios as a first-pass triage signal, not financial advice. Always do your own research.

Is my watchlist data private?

Yes. There is no backend server. Your watchlist and settings live in your browser's local storage; the extension only fetches public market data for the symbols you saved.
