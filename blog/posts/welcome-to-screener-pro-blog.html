Screener Pro is a free browser extension (available for Chrome and Firefox) that turns every browser tab into a market workspace. Instead of juggling Screener.in tabs, Yahoo Finance pages, news sites and spreadsheets, you get a live watchlist, a global ticker tape, market data for 10 economies and smart stock verdicts — all without leaving the page you're on.

This guide walks through everything the extension does, with screenshots, and who each feature helps most.

1. Live watchlist in the side panel

The heart of Screener Pro is the watchlist that lives in your browser's side panel. Search any ticker once and it stays there with a live price, day's change, sparkline chart, market cap, P/E and other key ratios — updating through the day.

Indian stocks show ₹ values from NSE/BSE data, while US and global stocks keep their native currencies. Green and red movement highlights make it obvious at a glance what's moving. Who it helps: anyone who checks prices ten times a day — one glance replaces three open tabs.

2. A ticker tape on every website

A slim, live ticker tape runs across your screen on every site you visit — indices and your own watchlist scrolling by, with pause, 0.5x–3.0x speed control, and per-site disable when you want a page clean.

Who it helps: day traders and market-watchers who want ambient awareness of the market while reading news, researching, or working.

3. Markets tab: 10 economies, gainers, losers, macro

The Markets tab covers the USA, India, UK, Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Germany, France, Australia and Canada. For each economy you get top gainers and losers plus editable macro tiles — inflation, unemployment, GDP growth and GDP per capita, powered by World Bank data with links to the source.

Who it helps: investors diversifying beyond India, and anyone who wants to connect macro trends (rate decisions, GDP prints) to what's happening in their portfolio.

4. Smart verdicts and price alerts

Every stock gets an instant, color-coded health check built from its P/E and ROCE — undervalued, overvalued or high risk — so you can triage a watchlist in seconds before doing deeper research. Set custom price alerts and get notified when a stock crosses your levels.

Who it helps: beginners learning what "cheap" really means, and busy investors who can't stare at charts all day.

5. Add stocks from anywhere

Reading about a company on any website? Select its name, right-click, and send it straight to your watchlist — no copy-pasting tickers.

Log in to Screener.in in the same browser and the extension also syncs your customized ratios. Who it helps: researchers who discover ideas while reading and never want to lose one again.

6. Portfolios, export, news and dark mode

  • Multiple portfolios — separate long-term holdings from swing trades, with drag-and-drop ordering.
  • CSV export — download your whole watchlist with ratios to Excel in one click.
  • News feed — the latest relevant financial headlines for the stocks you actually hold.
  • Dark mode — easy on the eyes during late-night research sessions.

Your data stays yours

There is no backend server and no account to create. Your watchlist and settings live in your browser's local storage. The extension simply fetches public market data from Yahoo Finance, Screener.in and similar sources based on the symbols you saved.

Getting started

  • Install Screener Pro from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.
  • Pin it, open the side panel, and search your first ticker.
  • Press Ctrl+Shift+S anytime to jump back to your watchlist.

That's it — two minutes from install to a live market workspace. Future posts on this blog will go deeper: what P/E and ROCE really tell you, how to read the macro tiles, and how investors use each feature day to day.
