Effortless Sorting Across Key Financial Metrics

Whether you are scouting for the highest conviction large caps or screening for undervalued compounders, you can now toggle between ascending and descending sort orders in a single click:

  • Symbol (A → Z & Z → A): Click the Symbol header to alphabetise your watchlist. Perfect when verifying holdings or checking constituent lists against external brokerage portfolios.
  • Market Price (↑ / ↓): Sort high-to-low or low-to-high to quickly isolate which stocks are making major price moves or trading near key psychological round numbers.
  • P/E & Dynamic Ratio Column (↑ / ↓): Compare valuations on the fly. Clicking the ratio column header sorts by the active ratio—whether you have selected P/E, Dividend Yield, ROCE, ROE, or Price-to-Book (P/B). Changing the dropdown changes the underlying metric without breaking your sort orientation.
  • Market Cap (↑ / ↓): Full unit-aware parsing handles trillion-dollar titans ($T), billion-dollar giants ($B), and Indian Crores (&#8377; Cr) seamlessly, allowing you to rank holdings from mega-cap safety to high-beta small caps.

Non-Destructive Manual Drag Reordering

Sorting is purely visual: your underlying custom portfolio order remains intact whenever you clear the sort indicator or drag an item using the grab handle. This gives you analytical agility without losing your curated list arrangement.
