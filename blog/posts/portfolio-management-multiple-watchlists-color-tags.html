Dedicated Portfolios for Every Trading Strategy

With the unified header switcher, your active portfolio doubles as an interactive selector. Switch between "Core Holdings", "Dividend Compounders", "Earnings Watch", and "Swing Setups" in a fraction of a second without losing your page scroll.

  • Quick Creation & Renaming: Tap the + button to spin up a new portfolio, or the ✓ / ✎ pencil icon to rename an existing list immediately.
  • Bulk Management: Toggle into Select mode to select dozens of tickers at once. Move, copy, or purge multiple positions across portfolios in a single action.
  • Colour-Coded Tagging: Assign green (Entry Target), amber (Watching), or red (Exit Flag) dots to individual tickers. Use the tag filter dropdown to isolate only stocks awaiting buy triggers.

Exporting to Excel & Spreadsheets

Need to run DCF models or tax-loss harvesting analysis? Click the compact CSV pill in the header to download a clean spreadsheet export complete with tickers, company names, prices, P/E multiples, market caps, ROCE figures, and assigned tags.
