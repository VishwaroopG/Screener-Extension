Bottom line: The most painful friction when trying a new market tool is re-entering your watchlist ticker by ticker. Ticker Screener eliminates that chore with multi-source import detection.

Three Zero-Friction Import Paths

  1. Detect from Active Tab: Keep your Zerodha Kite holdings, Screener.in screen, or TradingView watchlist open in any browser tab. Click Import → Detect from open tab. The extension scans rendered tickers locally in memory and displays a verified preview checklist.
  2. Paste Tickers Directly: Copy-paste any comma, space, or newline-separated string (e.g. RELIANCE, INFY, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA). The engine automatically trims whitespace and standardises symbols.
  3. CSV & Text File Upload: Export your portfolio from your preferred brokerage and drop the CSV into the import modal. Duplicate symbols already present in your active portfolio are automatically skipped.

Security and Privacy by Design

Unlike third-party trackers that demand API tokens or broker credentials, Ticker Screener runs 100% locally inside your browser client. Tab detection only runs when you explicitly click the Detect button, and data never leaves your device.

Can I import both US and Indian tickers together?

Yes. Ticker Screener supports hybrid watchlists. Indian stocks quote in INR with Screener.in deep links, while US equities stream in USD with Yahoo Finance depth.
