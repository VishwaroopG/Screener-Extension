Bottom line: v2.7.0 adds Fundamentals Depth to every stock search. Search RELIANCE, HDFCBANK or INFY and you now get a free checklist score, SWOT, forensic red-flags, promoter/FII/DII shareholding with trend, concall transcripts and one-click research links - the depth Tickertape locks behind Pro, now inside your side panel.

What shows up on every search?

  • Checklist score /10 - pros minus cons from Screener.in, plus quality kickers for ROCE > 15%, P/E < 25 and ROE > 12%.
  • SWOT 2x2 - Strengths and Weaknesses straight from Screener pros/cons; Opportunities and Threats derived from your ratios (valuation, ROCE base, payout).
  • Forensic red-flags - pledge, contingent, debtor, borrowing keywords plus rules: ROE < 10%, high P/E with low ROCE, yield < 0.5%.
  • Shareholding - promoters, FIIs, DIIs, public for the latest quarter with multi-quarter trend in pp. Pledge % links out for login-gated verification.
  • Concalls - latest 3 transcripts with Transcript / PPT / AI Summary buttons.
  • Research + PDF - Screener report, annual reports, Tickertape page, Trendlyne + Superstars, and a PDF button that prints the card.

Best for: Indian equity investors who want Tickertape/Screener depth without opening five tabs or paying for Pro.

How do I use it?

Open the side panel, type a ticker in Search Ticker to Analyze or Add… and hit Search. The Fundamentals Depth card renders below the ratios table once Screener.in responds - no extra click. Global stocks (AAPL, NVDA) keep the same layout with Yahoo fundamentals.

Where does the data come from?

Indian equities parse live from screener.in/company/TICKER/consolidated/: #analysis .pros/.cons, #shareholding table.data-table, .concalls ul.list-links. Ratios you already have (P/E, ROCE, ROE, yield) feed the score and flags. Nothing is stored - it is parsed at search time and rendered inline.

Frequently asked questions

Is this financial advice?

No. The score and flags are triage from public checklist items and ratios. Always do your own research.

Why is pledged % a link, not a number?

Screener gates detailed pledge/shareholder names behind login. We show the trend and deep-link to verify.

Does it work for US stocks?

Yes - the card renders with Yahoo ratios and research shortcuts. Full pros/cons + shareholding parsing applies to Indian (Screener-covered) tickers.
