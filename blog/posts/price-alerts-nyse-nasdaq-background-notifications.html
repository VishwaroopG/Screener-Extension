Bottom line: price alerts in Ticker Screener run on background alarms and fire desktop notifications the moment a NYSE or Nasdaq threshold crosses - even with the side panel closed and while you read other sites. Set them at levels you chose calmly, not emotionally.

What you can alert on

  • Price levels: breakout above resistance, breakdown below support, round-number reclaim.
  • Percentage swings: catch >5% movers without staring at quotes - Smart Focus blue-dots them too.
  • Valuation-adjacent levels: pre-compute the price where yield or P/E hits your target, alert there instead of watching daily.

Best for: anyone who missed a move because the tab was closed.

Three alert setups that actually work

  1. Earnings straddle: one alert above the options-implied move, one below. Delete the survivor after the print.
  2. Breakout confirmation: alert at the 52-week high, decide after the close - never chase 5% beyond trigger.
  3. Dividend target: alert at the price where Div Yld hits your hurdle (e.g. 4%), then check payout safety before acting.

Background alarms, not open tabs

Every price tick is evaluated against your thresholds in the background as prices stream in. Movement highlights paint the ticker row green or red the instant a condition is met, and the notification fires whether the panel is open or not. Mute per stock when a name gets noisy; bulk-manage alerts across portfolios without losing scroll.

Frequently asked questions

Do alerts work when the panel is closed?

Yes - background alarms keep evaluating ticks and push desktop notifications.

How many alerts can I set?

Set them per stock across portfolios; mute or clear in bulk with Select mode.

Where do I get the extension?

Free on the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons - search Ticker Screener.
