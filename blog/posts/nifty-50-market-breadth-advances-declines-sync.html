How to Interpret Market Breadth

When advance/decline ratios sit above 3:1 (e.g., 38 advances vs 12 declines), buying pressure is broad-based and rallies have strong momentum. Ratios below 1:3 indicate pervasive selling where even defensives face margin pressures.
