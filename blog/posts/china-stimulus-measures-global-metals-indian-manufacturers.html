Bottom line: China consumes over 50% of the world's refined copper, aluminium, and seaborne iron ore. When the People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the Ministry of Finance deploy bazooka fiscal and monetary stimuli, global commodity cycles pivot overnight.

The Re-Rating of Global Metal Equities

Years of property sector stagnation in China created deflationary pressures, prompting Chinese steel and chemical mills to dump excess capacity onto global markets at distressed prices. Aggressive fiscal easing alters this equilibrium:

  1. Domestic Absorption: Beijing's infrastructure spending and home-renovation subsidies absorb domestic industrial output, curtailing aggressive export dumping.
  2. Global Commodity Price Surge: LME copper, zinc, and alumina prices experience immediate speculative and industrial bidding, elevating global selling prices.
  3. Indian Steel & Aluminium Multiples: Domestic producers like Tata Steel, JSW Steel, and Hindalco benefit from both rising international benchmarks and strong domestic infrastructure demand under India's national capital expenditure program.
