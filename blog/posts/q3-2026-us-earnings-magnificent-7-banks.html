The October 2026 setup

Context matters: the Fed hiked to 3.75–4.00% on September 17 with one more hike signalled, the 10-year sits above 5.10%, Brent holds above $100 and September saw heavy foreign selling in EM. US large-caps enter earnings with elevated bars — revenue beats will matter more than EPS beats engineered by buybacks. Any guide-down citing enterprise softness or credit costs will move the stock 5–10% same-day.

What to watch by group

  • Magnificent 7: cloud growth re-acceleration, AI capex vs free cash flow, ad-spend resilience. The market forgives heavy capex only if revenue follows within two quarters.
  • US banks (JPM, BofA, Citi, Wells): net interest income trajectory, deposit costs, office-loan reserves, investment-banking rebound. Watch deposit beta instead of stickiness ratios.
  • Everyone else: pricing power (gross margin holding with oil above $100?), inventory discipline, and buyback pace as rates bite.

Best for: US investors holding index funds who want to know which two or three prints actually move the S&P 500.

An earnings-week checklist

  1. Compare to whisper, not just consensus: options-implied moves tell you the real bar.
  2. Read the guide first: a 2% beat with lowered Q4 guide is a sell-the-news setup.
  3. Check breadth that day: if NYSE + Nasdaq advance/decline is negative while your stock beats, the market is telling you something about duration.
  4. Do not trade the first 15 minutes: let the call, not the headline, set the price.
