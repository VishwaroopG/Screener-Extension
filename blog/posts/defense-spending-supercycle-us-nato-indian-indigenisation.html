Bottom line: Decades of the post-Cold War 'peace dividend' have ended. Geopolitical tensions across Eastern Europe, the Indo-Pacific, and the Middle East have triggered a multi-decade global defense capex supercycle.

The Dual Defense Expansion: Western Giants and Indian Champions

Defense spending is structurally insulated from retail economic slowdowns because budgets are statutory, multi-year sovereign commitments:

  • US & NATO Re-Armament: NATO members are finally meeting and exceeding their 2% GDP defense spending guidelines. Order books for Lockheed Martin, RTX, General Dynamics, and European defense primes are at all-time backlogs across missile defense, autonomous drones, and naval shipbuilding.
  • India's Defense Indigenisation (Aatmanirbhar Bharat): The Ministry of Defence's phased positive indigenisation lists have systematically banned the import of hundreds of military platforms. Domestic public and private defense players (HAL, Bharat Electronics, Solar Industries, Mazagon Dock, Data Patterns) enjoy 3x-5x order-book-to-bill ratios.
  • Export Ambitions: Indian defense exports have surged over tenfold in the last decade, supplying indigenous artillery, radar, and missile systems (e.g. BrahMos) to Southeast Asian and African partners.

Valuation Discipline

While multi-year earnings visibility is rock-solid, sharp price appreciation requires investors to monitor order execution timelines and working capital efficiency rather than chasing momentum at any valuation.
