Bottom line: Microchips are the new crude oil of the 21st century. With over 90% of advanced sub-5nm logic chips produced in Taiwan, global powers are investing hundreds of billions to construct redundant, resilient semiconductor manufacturing corridors.

The Global Triad: US, Taiwan & India

Geopolitical de-risking is reshaping capital allocation across global tech equities:

  • US CHIPS Act ($52B+): Massive subsidies are driving advanced wafer fabs in Arizona, Ohio, and Texas by Intel, TSMC, and Samsung, boosting US semiconductor equipment leaders like Applied Materials, Lam Research, and KLA.
  • Taiwan's Enduring Fab Hegemony: Despite geopolitical posturing, TSMC maintains an unmatched technological lead in advanced packaging and extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography yield efficiency.
  • India's Semiconductor Mission ($10B Incentive Program): Rather than competing immediately in high-risk sub-3nm logic, India has focused on strategic Out-sourced Semiconductor Assembly and Testing (OSAT), compound semiconductors, and mature node manufacturing (e.g. Tata Electronics in Gujarat and Assam, Murugappa Group, and Micron packaging facilities).

Investment Implications

Keep a close eye on global semiconductor equipment makers, clean-room infrastructure suppliers, and Indian electronic manufacturing service (EMS) compounders capturing structural tailwinds.
