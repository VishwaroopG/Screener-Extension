Bottom line: three views - ticker tape, heatmap, breadth - tell you in 30 seconds whether a US rally is broad or seven stocks wide. Tape for ambient prices, heatmap for who is really moving, breadth for participation. All three ship in Ticker Screener's Markets tab.

1 - Tape: ambient awareness

The silk tape scrolls S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow and your watchlist across every site you visit - same px/sec on 60Hz and 120Hz screens, seamless loop both directions, pause and 0.5x–3.0x speed, per-site disable. Flash highlights are background-only so ticks never jitter mid-scroll. Read it the way pilots read instruments: green day, red day, or chop - then move on.

Best for: staying oriented while researching instead of alt-tabbing to quotes.

2 - Heatmap: who is actually moving

Every index stock becomes a tile sized by market cap and shaded by move - red gains, green losses, grey flat (extension convention). Hover any tile for exact % and price; click for sparkline, P/E, market cap, AI verdict and 1M–MAX chart. A rally where mega-cap tiles glow while the rest sits grey is narrow by definition.

3 - Breadth + mood: participation and sentiment

Advance/decline breadth shows combined NYSE + Nasdaq totals (WSJ-sourced, 10-min cache) - true market participation, not a partial count. Above it sits the Market Mood gauge: live CNN Fear & Greed for the USA (Tickertape MMI for India), hover arcs for zones, ⓘ for factors. Breadth weak + mood euphoric is the classic late-stage warning; breadth strong + mood fearful is where bottoms form.

The 30-second routine

  1. Glance at tape: indices green or red, watchlist with them or diverging?
  2. Open heatmap: mega-caps only, or rows of mid-caps participating?
  3. Check breadth vs mood: participation confirming price, sentiment confirming or contradicting?

Frequently asked questions

Tape vs heatmap - which first?

Tape for the day's direction, heatmap for its composition, breadth for conviction. Thirty seconds, in that order.

Where does breadth data come from?

WSJ Markets Diary for the USA, Moneycontrol Nifty 500 for India, cached 10 minutes to stay fresh without hammering sources.

Where do I get the extension?

Free on the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons - search Ticker Screener.
