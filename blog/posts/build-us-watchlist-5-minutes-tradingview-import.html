Bottom line: you can build a live US watchlist - S&P 500 names, Nasdaq growers, dividend payers - in about five minutes: detect tickers from an open TradingView or Screener.in tab, paste a list, or upload a CSV. Duplicates merge automatically, prices stream in dollars, and tags plus Smart Focus do the sorting from there.

Step 1 - Import (pick one path)

  1. Detect from open tab: open TradingView, Kite or Screener.in, click Detect - tickers are read only at that moment, shown in a checkbox preview with live counts, and added only after you approve. Login-walled pages work because detection reads the rendered tab.
  2. Paste a list: AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, JPM - any separator works, tickers auto-resolve.
  3. Upload CSV/TXT: dump holdings from any broker export; duplicates skip, the active portfolio merges cleanly.

Best for: anyone migrating holdings who dreads retyping 40 tickers.

Step 2 - Organise with tags and portfolios

Split by job: "Core", "Dividends", "Breakouts", "Q3 Earnings". Dot-tag by conviction or theme with color tags, filter by tag in one click, and use the portfolio dropdown beside Your Watchlist to switch contexts without losing scroll. Bulk Select mode adds header Select-All for tagging, moving or deleting dozens at once.

Step 3 - Switch ratios, set alerts, done

Tap the P/E header to flip the column to Div Yld, ROCE, ROE or P/B - choice persists, formatting stays Screener-style. Let Smart Focus blue-dot big >5% moves, unusual swings and 52-week edges, hit Focus for firing stocks only, and set background alerts so desktop notifications fire even with the panel closed.

Frequently asked questions

Does import read my data in the background?

No. Tickers are read only when you click Detect, shown for confirmation, and added only after approval.

Can I keep Indian and US stocks together?

Yes - US quotes stay in dollars, Indian ones in rupees. No silent conversion, portfolios carry across economies.

Where do I get the extension?

Free on the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons - search Ticker Screener.
