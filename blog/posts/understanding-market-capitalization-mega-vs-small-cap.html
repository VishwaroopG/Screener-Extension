Bottom line: Market capitalisation is the total market value of a company's outstanding shares. Understanding market cap tiers is essential for calibrating risk, expected volatility, and portfolio allocation.

Market Capitalisation Tiers Defined

  • Mega-Cap ($200B+ / >&#8377;2 Lakh Cr): Global leaders like Apple, Microsoft, Reliance, and TCS. These cash-generative powerhouses anchor conservative portfolios with deep institutional liquidity and low default risk.
  • Large-Cap ($10B–$200B / &#8377;50,000 Cr–&#8377;2 Lakh Cr): Established blue-chips with proven competitive advantages and resilient balance sheets.
  • Mid-Cap ($2B–$10B / &#8377;10,000 Cr–&#8377;50,000 Cr): The sweet spot for long-term compounders. High growth potential combined with established operational track records.
  • Small-Cap & Micro-Cap (<$2B / <&#8377;10,000 Cr): Emerging challengers. High upside potential paired with sharp drawdowns, illiquidity risk, and wider bid-ask spreads.
