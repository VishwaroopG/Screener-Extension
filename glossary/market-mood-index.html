Market Mood Index (MMI)

Tickertape's live 0–100 sentiment gauge for Indian stocks — the desi Fear & Greed, from extreme fear to extreme greed.

The Market Mood Index compresses volatility, momentum and demand signals for Indian equities into a single live number. Under ~30 reads fear, above ~70 greed.

Like all sentiment tools it works best against the crowd: fear zones favour staggered buying quality, greed zones favour booking profits and tightening stops.

Example

MMI under 20 during FII-selling panics has repeatedly marked zones where SIPs and staggered large-cap buys compounded well.

Frequently asked questions

Is MMI a buy/sell signal?

No — a sentiment thermometer. Combine it with valuations, breadth and your asset allocation before acting.

Related terms

Fear & Greed Index

CNN's 0–100 gauge of US market sentiment across seven inputs. Extreme fear often marks opportunity; extreme greed marks danger.

Advance-Decline Ratio

Winners divided by losers across the market. Above 1 means broad gains; below 1, broad pain — breadth in one number.

Nifty 50

India's benchmark: 50 large stocks across 14+ sectors on the NSE. When India 'is up 1%', this is usually the number.
