Nifty 50

India's benchmark: 50 large stocks across 14+ sectors on the NSE. When India 'is up 1%', this is usually the number.

The Nifty 50 tracks fifty large, liquid Indian stocks weighted by market value. Banks and IT dominate, so financial results season moves the index disproportionately.

Foreign investors benchmark India allocations to it, and derivatives on it are among the world's most traded - expiry-day swings often start here.

Example

A 200-point Nifty move on HDFC Bank and Reliance earnings day can happen while most small-caps fall - index versus portfolio divergence is normal.

Frequently asked questions

Nifty vs Sensex - which to follow?

Both mirror India's large-caps and move together; Nifty (NSE, 50 stocks) leads derivatives, Sensex (BSE, 30 stocks) leads headlines. Either works as a benchmark.

Related terms

Sensex

BSE's 30-stock benchmark and India's oldest market barometer. Asia's first exchange, still the headline number.

Index

A rule-based basket measuring a market slice - Nifty 50, S&P 500, gold prices. The benchmark everything gets judged against.

Market Cap

Price times shares outstanding: the market's price tag for the whole company. Defines large, mid and small caps.
