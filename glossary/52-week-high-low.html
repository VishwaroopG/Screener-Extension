52-Week High / Low

A stock's highest and lowest price over the past year. Breakouts signal strength; breakdowns signal trouble - both demand volume proof.

The 52-week high/low frames today's price against a full year of trading. Stocks near highs carry momentum and overhead-supply tests; stocks near lows carry turnaround hopes or terminal decline.

New highs on rising volume confirm institutional accumulation; new lows on volume confirm distribution. Price alone, without volume, is half the story.

Example

A railway stock breaking its 52-week high after a Rs 10,000-crore order win differs completely from one drifting to highs on thin volume - same price, opposite meaning.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy 52-week highs?

Momentum works until it doesn't - buy highs with volume confirmation and stop-losses, never on hope. Breakdowns deserve even stricter exits.

Related terms

Circuit Breaker

Exchange price bands that freeze trading after sharp moves - usually 2%, 5%, 10% or 20% per stock.

Market Cap

Price times shares outstanding: the market's price tag for the whole company. Defines large, mid and small caps.

Advance-Decline Ratio

Winners divided by losers across the market. Above 1 means broad gains; below 1, broad pain - breadth in one number.
