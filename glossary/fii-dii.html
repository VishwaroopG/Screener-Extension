FII & DII

Foreign vs domestic institutional investors. Their tug-of-war sets India's market tone - FIIs flee, DIIs absorb.

FIIs (foreign institutional investors) now called FPIs, deploy global capital into India; DIIs (domestic institutions) - mutual funds, insurers, NPS - invest local savings. Daily buy/sell figures move sentiment more than fundamentals short-term.

The structural shift: monthly SIP flows made DIIs persistent buyers, cushioning FII exits that once crashed markets. Watch the net, not one side.

Example

Rs 2.45 lakh crore of FPI outflows in a risk-off year were absorbed without a bear market - DII SIPs did the heavy lifting.

Related terms

Nifty 50

India's benchmark: 50 large stocks across 14+ sectors on the NSE. When India 'is up 1%', this is usually the number.

Index

A rule-based basket measuring a market slice - Nifty 50, S&P 500, gold prices. The benchmark everything gets judged against.

Market Cap

Price times shares outstanding: the market's price tag for the whole company. Defines large, mid and small caps.
