Order Book

Confirmed-but-unexecuted contracts - future revenue already signed. The visibility metric for rail, defence and EPC stocks.

An order book totals signed contracts awaiting execution. Divide it by yearly revenue ('book-to-bill' or coverage) to see how many years of work are already secured.

Quality beats size: government orders pay slowly but surely, private orders pay faster but cancel easier. Watch execution (conversion to revenue), not just addition.

Example

A Rs 40,000-crore book on Rs 15,000-crore revenue = 2.7 years of visibility. If quarterly execution stalls, the book is decoration.

Frequently asked questions

Order book vs order inflow?

Inflow is new wins this period; book is the cumulative backlog. Inflow accelerates the story, book sustains it.

Related terms

EPS

Earnings per share: the company's yearly profit divided by share count. The 'E' inside P/E.

Market Cap

Price times shares outstanding: the market's price tag for the whole company. Defines large, mid and small caps.

P/E Ratio

Price-to-earnings: what you pay for each rupee or dollar of yearly profit. The starting lens for cheap vs expensive.
