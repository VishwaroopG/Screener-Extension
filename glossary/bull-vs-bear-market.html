Bull vs Bear Market

Bull = sustained +20% rise on optimism; bear = −20% fall on pessimism. Regimes decide strategy more than stock picks.

A bull market rises 20%+ amid confidence, liquidity and earnings growth; a bear market falls 20%+ amid fear, tightening or recession. The labels describe sustained regimes, not single weeks.

Strategy follows regime: bulls reward buying dips and riding momentum; bears reward cash, quality balance sheets and staggered entries. Most investor damage comes from using bull tactics in bear markets.

Example

2020–21's liquidity bull forgave every mistake; 2022's rate-shock bear punished them. Same investors, opposite outcomes - regime did the work.

Related terms

Market Mood Index (MMI)

Tickertape's live 0–100 sentiment gauge for Indian stocks - the desi Fear & Greed, from extreme fear to extreme greed.

Fear & Greed Index

CNN's 0–100 gauge of US market sentiment across seven inputs. Extreme fear often marks opportunity; extreme greed marks danger.

Advance-Decline Ratio

Winners divided by losers across the market. Above 1 means broad gains; below 1, broad pain - breadth in one number.
