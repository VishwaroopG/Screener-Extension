Nasdaq

The US tech-heavy exchange and its indices (Composite, Nasdaq 100). Growth's home - faster rallies, harder falls.

Nasdaq is both an exchange and shorthand for its indices. The Composite covers 3,000+ stocks; the Nasdaq 100 holds the top 100 non-financials and underlies the QQQ ETF.

Tech concentration means rate sensitivity: when yields spike, long-duration growth valuations compress and Nasdaq leads declines.

Example

A 1% Fed-surprise repricing often moves Nasdaq 2%+ while the Dow barely blinks - duration explains the gap.

In Ticker Screener

The Markets tab shows Nasdaq beside S&P 500 and Dow with whole-market NYSE + Nasdaq breadth, so narrow tech rallies can't fool you.

Frequently asked questions

Nasdaq vs NYSE - what's the difference?

Two rival exchanges: Nasdaq is electronic and tech-heavy; NYSE hosts more old-economy giants. Listings, not quality, separate them.

Related terms

S&P 500

500 large US companies, cap-weighted. The default scoreboard for American equities - and your global benchmark.

Index

A rule-based basket measuring a market slice - Nifty 50, S&P 500, gold prices. The benchmark everything gets judged against.

ETF

Exchange-traded fund: a basket of stocks, bonds or gold bought like one share. Instant diversification, tiny fees.
