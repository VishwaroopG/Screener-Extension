Circuit Breaker

Exchange price bands that freeze trading after sharp moves - usually 2%, 5%, 10% or 20% per stock.

Circuit breakers (price bands) cap how far a stock can move in a day. Hit the upper circuit and buyers queue with no sellers; hit lower and you cannot exit - the classic penny-stock trap.

Bands revise with volatility and corporate actions. Index-level market-wide halts (Nifty/Sensex ±10/15/20%) pause the whole market instead.

Example

A 20% lower-circuit lock means your sell order sits unfilled all day while the quote blinks red - liquidity risk made visible.

Frequently asked questions

Can circuits be revised mid-day?

Exchanges revise bands periodically; special pre-open sessions handle520%+ corporate-action adjustments. Check the day's applicable filter before trading.

Related terms

Nifty 50

India's benchmark: 50 large stocks across 14+ sectors on the NSE. When India 'is up 1%', this is usually the number.

52-Week High / Low

A stock's highest and lowest price over the past year. Breakouts signal strength; breakdowns signal trouble - both demand volume proof.

Market Cap

Price times shares outstanding: the market's price tag for the whole company. Defines large, mid and small caps.
