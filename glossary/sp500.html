S&P 500

500 large US companies, cap-weighted. The default scoreboard for American equities - and your global benchmark.

The S&P 500 holds five hundred leading US companies chosen by committee for size, liquidity and profitability. Roughly a third of its weight sits in the Magnificent 7.

Most active US funds underperform it over a decade, which is why it anchors global portfolios - including those of Indian investors diversifying abroad.

Example

When Nvidia reports, the S&P 500 often moves more on that one print than on the other 499 combined - concentration in action.

Related terms

Index

A rule-based basket measuring a market slice - Nifty 50, S&P 500, gold prices. The benchmark everything gets judged against.

ETF

Exchange-traded fund: a basket of stocks, bonds or gold bought like one share. Instant diversification, tiny fees.

Market Cap

Price times shares outstanding: the market's price tag for the whole company. Defines large, mid and small caps.
