What actually moves a stock price

Earnings, interest rates, fund flows and sentiment - the four forces behind every price move, and which one dominates yours.

1. Earnings. Over years, prices follow profits. Quarterly results reset expectations - guidance (what management promises next) moves stocks more than the reported quarter itself.

2. Interest rates. When the US Fed or RBI raises rates, future profits get discounted harder and growth stocks fall first. This single channel explains most 2022 damage - see Fed to FII.

3. Fund flows. FII selling and DII SIP buying tug Indian large-caps daily. Flows dominate months; earnings dominate years. Know which game your holding period plays - FII & DII explained.

4. Sentiment. Greed zones stretch multiples, fear zones compress them. Gauges like the Market Mood Index tell you which crowd you are trading against.

Do this now: pick your biggest holding and assign its last 10% move to one of the four forces. If you cannot, you are trading a story you haven't verified.

Frequently asked questions

Good results but stock fell - why?

Expectations beat reality: the 'whisper number' exceeded guidance, or the market sold the broader sector. Price reacts to surprise, not quality.
