5 beginner mistakes that lose money (and fixes)

Tips, averaging down, penny chasing, no stop-losses, all-in bets - the five classic beginner errors with a one-line fix each.

1. Acting on tips. By the time a tip reaches you, early buyers need your exit. Fix: no position without your own two-line thesis - what it earns, why it grows.

2. Averaging down blindly. Adding to losers doubles exposure to whatever is going wrong. Fix: average only on pre-planned levels with fresh thesis intact - never to 'recover' fast. Our penny-stock screen shows why falling knives usually keep falling.

3. No position sizing. A 40% portfolio bet that halves needs a 67% gain elsewhere to recover. Fix: cap any single stock at 5–10% until you have three years of process.

4. No stop-loss or review date. Hope is not risk management. Fix: every buy gets an exit price (thesis broken) and a review date (thesis check).

5. Confusing bull markets with skill. Rising tides certify geniuses. Fix: benchmark every return against the Nifty 50 or S&P 500 - outperformance net of risk is the only grade.
