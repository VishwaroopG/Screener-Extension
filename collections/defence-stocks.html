Defence stocks in India

Defence stocks in India - HAL, BEL, Mazagon Dock, Cochin Shipyard and more - with screening criteria, order-book metrics, risks and FAQs.

IndiaDefencePSUMake in India

Import embargo lists, a rising defence budget and export orders have given Indian defence stocks a multi-year order pipeline. The theme splits into platforms (HAL aircraft, Mazagon and Cochin shipyards), electronics (BEL radars and systems) and private precision players (missiles, drones, components).

Defence rewards patience: development cycles run for years, then execution compounds. Screen on order-book coverage, indigenisation content and export share - and size positions knowing these stocks correct 20–30% whenever euphoria peaks.

How to screen defence stocks yourself

  • Order book of 3x revenue or more - shipyards and HAL routinely carry multi-year backlogs; that visibility justifies premium multiples.
  • Indigenisation content rising - higher domestic value-add means better margins and policy protection.
  • Export share - exports de-risk dependence on domestic budget cycles; even 10–15% changes the story.
  • ROCE above 18% with positive free cash flow - defence advances can flatter profits while cash lags.
  • Delivery milestones, not headlines - track commissioning and handover dates, the events that actually convert backlog to revenue.

Risks to watch

  • Paying 60x+ for private names on hope. Small defence stocks can be excellent businesses and terrible investments at peak multiples.
  • Ignoring execution delays. A submarine delayed two years is two years of missing cash flow - read management commentary on timelines.
  • Geopolitical spike-chasing. Conflict headlines lift the whole basket intraday and fade just as fast.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best defence stock in India?

HAL and BEL anchor most portfolios for scale and dividends; shipyards add growth with lumpier execution; private names add torque with higher risk. There is no single best - match the pick to your risk appetite.

Why are defence stocks falling or so volatile?

Long contracts plus high multiples equal volatility: any delay, budget signal or promoter stake sale reprices years of expectations at once. Volatility is the entry fee for this theme.

Do defence stocks pay dividends?

The PSU majors (HAL, BEL) generally do. Private precision players usually reinvest. Check dividend yield before assuming income.
