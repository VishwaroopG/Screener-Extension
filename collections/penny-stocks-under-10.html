Penny stocks under Rs 10

Penny stocks under Rs 10 - a safe screening framework, red flags that kill capital, and the only metrics that matter before you touch them.

IndiaPenny StocksHigh RiskScreening

A Rs 7 stock that doubles feels like free money - until you learn why it trades at Rs 7. Sub-Rs-10 stocks are usually cheap for structural reasons: heavy debt, pledged promoter shares, years of losses, or businesses the market has stopped trusting. A few are genuine turnarounds; most are traps with good storytelling.

This page deliberately has no fixed buy list - penny membership changes weekly and any static list goes stale. Instead, here is the exact screen professionals run, the red flags that disqualify 9 out of 10 candidates, and how to track survivors live without staring at charts all day.

How to screen sub-Rs-10 stocks yourself

  • Market cap above Rs 500 crore - filters out illiquid shells where you cannot exit.
  • Positive operating cash flow in the last year - profits can be engineered; cash is harder to fake.
  • Promoter holding above 40% with zero pledging - pledged shares trigger forced selling spirals.
  • Debt-to-equity below 1 - leveraged pennies rarely survive rate cycles.
  • Delivery percentage above 50% - confirms real buyers, not intraday operator churn.

Risks to watch

  • Circuit-filter locks. Many pennies hit upper circuits you cannot buy and lower circuits you cannot sell - check the circuit filter before entering.
  • Bonus/split mirages. A split changes nothing fundamental; operators use the buzz to distribute.
  • SMS and social-media tips. Pump-and-dump schemes live in this segment. If the tip arrived free, you are the exit liquidity.
  • Averaging down. A falling penny is usually falling for a reason. Fixed position sizes, hard stop-losses, no exceptions.

Frequently asked questions

Can penny stocks make you rich?

Rarely, and survivorship bias hides the graveyard. For every multibagger penny, hundreds get suspended or fade to zero. Treat the segment as a lottery ticket allocation - small, losable, rules-based.

What is a safe quantity of penny stocks to buy?

Sizing matters more than picking: risk only what you can lose entirely, use stop-losses, and never let one penny exceed a few percent of your portfolio.

Why do penny stocks hit circuits every day?

Low floats plus speculative demand. Exchanges apply price bands (circuits) that freeze trading - which is exactly why exits fail when you need them most. See circuit breakers.
