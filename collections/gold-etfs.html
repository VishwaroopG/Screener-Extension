Gold ETFs in India and the US

Gold ETFs - GLD, IAU, SGOL, GoldBeES and more - expense ratios, tracking error, physical vs sovereign gold, and FAQs.

GoldETFIndiaUSSafe Haven

Gold ETFs let you own gold at market price without lockers, making charges or theft risk. Each unit tracks domestic gold prices (India) or spot bullion (US), and you buy and sell them like any stock - which also means you can watch them on the same tape as your equities.

Choosing between them comes down to three numbers: expense ratio (yearly fee), tracking error (how tightly it follows gold) and liquidity (spreads and volumes). US investors pick from giants like GLD and IAU; Indian investors compare GoldBeES-style ETFs against Sovereign Gold Bonds and physical gold.

How to screen gold ETFs yourself

  • Expense ratio under 0.40% (US) or 0.60% (India) - fees compound against you every year gold goes sideways.
  • Tracking error under 1% - the ETF should mirror gold, not the fund manager's timing calls.
  • Daily value traded above Rs 5 crore / $5 million - thin ETFs punish you with wide bid-ask spreads.
  • Physical backing for core holdings - synthetic or leveraged gold products are trading tools, not savings.

Risks to watch

  • Leveraged gold ETFs as investments. Daily-reset leverage decays over months - fine for a trade, ruinous as a holding.
  • Ignoring tax treatment. Indian gold ETF gains and SGB redemption rules differ materially; check current tax law before choosing.
  • Over-allocating. Gold hedges equity drawdowns but earns nothing; 5–15% is a hedge, 50% is a different portfolio.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better: gold ETF or physical gold?

ETFs win on liquidity, purity and zero making charges; physical wins for gifting and worst-case access. For investment purposes, ETFs (or sovereign bonds where available) are cleaner. See what an ETF is.

GLD vs IAU - which should I buy?

Same exposure, different wrappers: GLD has the tightest spreads for traders, IAU charges less per year for holders. Split by horizon, not hype.

Do gold ETFs pay dividends?

No - gold earns nothing, so there is nothing to distribute. Returns come purely from price appreciation.
