Railway stocks in India

Railway stocks in India - IRFC, RVNL, IRCTC, Titagarh and more - with screening criteria, order-book metrics to watch, risks and FAQs.

IndiaRailwaysPSUInfrastructure

India's railway capex has turned wagon makers, financiers and caterers into market favourites. The theme runs on three rails: government orders (Vande Bharat trainsets, freight corridors, station redevelopment), financing (IRFC funds most rolling-stock purchases) and services (IRCTC's ticketing and catering franchise).

Because order inflows are lumpy and valuations swing with Budget headlines, railway stocks reward investors who track order books, execution pace and dividend yield rather than chasing announcements. The screen below separates compounders from announcement rallies.

How to screen railway stocks yourself

  • Order book of at least 2x trailing revenue - two years of visible work before you pay a premium multiple.
  • Rising execution ratio - quarterly revenue divided by opening order book should trend up, not stall.
  • ROCE above 15% for manufacturers; financiers like IRFC are judged on margins and asset quality instead.
  • Dividend yield as cushion - PSU rail names often pay 2–4%, softening drawdowns between order cycles.
  • Stable government holding - offer-for-sale announcements routinely dent prices; check past floor-price history.

Risks to watch

  • Budget-day spikes. Allocations get priced in within hours; buying the headline candle usually means buying the top.
  • Announcements without order value. MOUs and lowest-bidder status are not revenue - wait for signed contract value.
  • Working-capital blowouts. EPC names funding receivables with debt see profits evaporate when rates rise.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the biggest railway stock in India?

By market value it is usually IRCTC or IRFC, depending on the cycle. Size is not a buy signal though - compare order-book coverage and return ratios before choosing.

Are railway stocks PSU stocks?

Most large ones are: IRFC, RVNL, IRCTC and IRCON are government-owned. That brings dependable dividends and big orders, but also offer-for-sale supply and policy-driven cycles.

What P/E is fair for railway stocks?

There is no fixed number - order-backed growers have traded anywhere from 20x to 60x earnings across cycles. Anchor on earnings growth and order coverage, not the multiple alone. See our P/E ratio guide.

P/E Ratio

Price-to-earnings: what you pay for each rupee or dollar of yearly profit. The starting lens for cheap vs expensive.

ROCE

Return on capital employed: how efficiently a company turns invested money into profit. Above 15–20% signals quality.

Dividend Yield

Annual dividend divided by share price. A 4% yield pays Rs 4 yearly per Rs 100 invested - if the payout lasts.

Market Cap

Price times shares outstanding: the market's price tag for the whole company. Defines large, mid and small caps.
