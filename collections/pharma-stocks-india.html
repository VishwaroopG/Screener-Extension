Pharma stocks in India

Pharma stocks in India - Sun Pharma, Cipla, Divi's, Lupin and more - USFDA screens, API vs formulations, risks and FAQs.

IndiaPharmaNifty PharmaHealthcare

Indian pharma sells generics to America, vaccines to the world and branded drugs at home. The money sits in three layers: formulations (Sun, Cipla, Lupin - high margin, USFDA-sensitive), APIs and CRDMO (Divi's - China's-plus-one beneficiary) and domestic brands (steady, price-controlled).

One regulatory letter can halve a stock, so screening here means reading USFDA inspection history, US revenue share and ANDA pipelines - not just P/E ratios.

How to screen pharma stocks yourself

  • Clean USFDA record (no OAI/Warning Letter on key plants) - check the FDA database, not just management commentary.
  • US revenue under 40% or diversified - concentration plus price erosion is the classic double blow.
  • ANDA pipeline with limited-competition launches - plain-vanilla generics erode 10–20% yearly on price.
  • Domestic growth above IPM average - India business stability funds US volatility.
  • Net cash or minimal debt - regulatory shocks hurt less when the balance sheet is clean.

Risks to watch

  • Single-plant dependence. If one facility makes 40% of US revenue, one Form 483 can define your year.
  • Ignoring price erosion. US generic prices fall most years - volume growth must outrun it.
  • Paying specialty multiples for generic earnings. Reserve premium P/Es for proven specialty or CRDMO franchises.

Frequently asked questions

Why do pharma stocks crash on USFDA news?

America is the profit pool. An import alert or warning letter can block a plant's US sales overnight - revenue, margins and multiples all reset together.

Sun Pharma vs Divi's - which is better?

Different sports: Sun is a specialty-formulations compounder, Divi's an API/CRDMO margin machine. Own the exposure you understand, or split across both layers.

What is an ANDA?

Abbreviated New Drug Application - the US approval that lets a generic maker sell a copy of a branded drug. Pending ANDAs are the visible pipeline; approvals are the revenue events.
