Privacy Policy — Ticker Screener

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Applies to: Ticker Screener Chrome Extension v2.6.1 and later · Developer / Data controller: Vishwaroop Galgali · Contact: vishwaroopg8@gmail.com

1. Overview

Ticker Screener (“the extension”) has a single purpose: to provide an integrated stock-market watchlist and real-time ticker tape so users can monitor stock prices, financial metrics, and market news across global and Indian markets directly within browser tabs and the side panel.

This policy, together with the extension’s in-product disclosures (permission prompts, Settings → Account & Sync, and the feedback form), comprehensively discloses how the extension collects, uses, handles, stores, and shares all user data, and all parties user data is shared with. There is no separate mobile app. The companion website at https://vishwaroopg.github.io/Screener-Extension/ and the /uninstall/ feedback page follow this same policy.

Short version: watchlists and settings stay on your device (and optionally in your own browser-profile sync storage). Ticker symbols you look up are sent to public market-data providers to fetch quotes. Feedback is sent to the developer only when you press Send. We do not sell data, run ads, use tracking SDKs, or build advertising profiles.

2. User Data Collection — what we collect

We collect only what is needed for the single purpose above. “Collection” includes data you type, data the extension reads via declared Chrome permissions, and query strings sent over the network to retrieve market data.

2.3 Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect browsing history, full URLs, page titles, page DOM/content, cookies, passwords, payment data, precise location, contacts, or files outside the optional feedback screenshot.
  • The ticker-tape content script (ticker_tape.js, matches <all_urls>) only renders your watchlist bar on pages you allow. It does not scrape, keylog, or exfiltrate page content, and it skips domains you disable.
  • We use no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, etc.), fingerprinting, or cross-site trackers in the extension.

3. How We Use User Data (handling & use)

  • Watchlists, portfolios, alerts, and UI preferences: used solely to render the side panel, ticker tape, charts, and desktop price alerts you configured, and to restore them across browser sessions.
  • Profile email (identity): used solely for local display in Settings → Account & Sync. Not used for authentication, tracking, or marketing.
  • Active-tab hostname (tabs): used solely to respect your per-website ticker enable/disable choice.
  • Highlighted text (contextMenus): used solely to resolve the selected company/ticker into a watchlist entry at the moment you click the menu item.
  • Ticker/search/macro queries: used solely to fetch the market data you requested (quotes, sparklines, ratios, news, macro indicators, holiday calendar).
  • Feedback name/email/message/screenshot: used solely to read and reply to your feedback. Email is optional and used only to reply if you provide it.
  • We do not use user data for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, or sale to third parties.

4. User Data Storage — where and how long

  • On-device storage (chrome.storage.local): watchlists (portfolios, screenerWatchlist, activePortfolioName), alerts (alerts, priceAlerts), preferences (theme, tape*, clockKeys, pinnedIndices, disabledDomains, overviewEconomy, macroTiles, syncEnabled), and performance caches (cachedData, marketIndices, macroStats, marketHolidays). Data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • Browser-profile sync (chrome.storage.sync): only the small preference subset listed above is mirrored to Chrome’s sync area when sync is enabled (default on; toggle in Settings). That copy is stored by Google as part of your browser-profile sync infrastructure (governed by Google’s privacy policy), not on a developer server. Bulky caches (cachedData, marketIndices, macroStats) intentionally stay local-only. The developer has no access to your sync area.
  • No developer server database: we operate no account system and no central database of watchlists. The only data that reaches the developer is feedback you explicitly send (see §5.7).
  • Retention: local data persists until you remove it (edit/delete in the panel, turn off sync, use “Clear data”, or uninstall the extension, which removes chrome.storage.local). Cached quotes/macro/holiday data refresh roughly every 5 minutes to 24 hours and are overwritten automatically.
  • Deletion: delete a watchlist/alert in the panel to remove it immediately; disable “Account & Sync” to stop sync mirroring (existing synced values remain in your Google account until you clear synced data via Chrome settings); uninstall to wipe local extension storage from the device.

6. Extension Permissions & Host Access — why each is needed

  • sidePanel — render the watchlist, quotes, charts, and metrics UI.
  • storage — save watchlists, alerts, and preferences locally and sync them via chrome.storage.sync.
  • alarms — run 5-minute background quote checks for your price alerts while the browser is open.
  • notifications — show desktop alerts when your own price/metric thresholds are crossed.
  • contextMenus — add the “Add to Screener Watchlist” right-click item for highlighted tickers.
  • tabs — read only the active tab’s domain to enforce your per-site ticker on/off list; send display updates to open tabs. No history/URL collection.
  • identity — read the signed-in profile email for local display only (Settings → Account & Sync).
  • Host permissions (screener.in, query1/query2.finance.yahoo.com, finviz.com, api.worldbank.org, news.google.com, formsubmit.co, vishwaroopg.github.io) — fetch exactly the data described in §5. Content-script matches (<all_urls>) render only the ticker-tape bar you configured; page content is never collected.

No remote code is used: all JavaScript/WebAssembly ships inside the extension package; no external <script>, external modules, or eval() of remote strings.

7. Data Security

  • All provider fetches use HTTPS. Feedback posts use HTTPS POST to FormSubmit.
  • Local data is protected by the browser/OS profile protections; sync copies inherit your Google account’s protections.
  • No method is 100% secure; avoid putting sensitive personal information in watchlist notes or feedback screenshots.

8. Your Controls & Choices

  • Per-website ticker control: disable the tape on any domain from the side panel.
  • Sync control: turn “Account & Sync” off at any time to keep everything local-only.
  • Delete control: remove watchlists, alerts, or notes in the panel; uninstall the extension to wipe local storage.
  • Feedback is entirely optional; you can use the whole extension without ever submitting it.

9. Children’s Privacy

The extension is a general-audience market utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact us for deletion if you believe a child provided feedback data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page and the Effective date when the extension’s data practices change, and will note material changes in the release notes. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
